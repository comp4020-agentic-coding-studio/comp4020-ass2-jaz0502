# Policies and support

<p class="lead">{frontmatter.description}</p>

## Late work

Work submitted after the deadline loses 5% of the available mark per day,
capped at five days. After five days a submission is marked as a fail unless
an extension has been granted. This applies per-assessment, not per-course —
a late Assignment 1 doesn't affect your Assignment 2 deadline.

## Extensions

Extensions are granted for illness, misadventure, or documented hardship, and
should be requested before the deadline where possible. Fieldwork-based
assessments (Assignments 1 and 2) can usually accommodate a short extension
without penalty, since the machine you're studying isn't going anywhere; the
final project's deadline is fixed against the semester's end and extensions
past it are rare.

## Academic integrity

The walkthroughs, incentive maps and teardowns in this course are meant to be
your own fieldwork — your own machine, your own transaction, your own
evidence. Discussing a machine with classmates is encouraged; submitting
someone else's fieldwork as your own is not. Declare any assistance, including
AI tools, in line with the university's standard declaration requirements.

## Getting help

Idris runs all twelve weekly tutorials and is the first port of call for
anything fieldwork-related — stuck on a machine, unsure what counts as a
strong walkthrough, need a second opinion before a studio. Marisol delivers
the lectures and sets the incentive question each one answers; email her for
anything conceptual that can wait until next week. Contact details for both
are on the [people](/people/) page.
