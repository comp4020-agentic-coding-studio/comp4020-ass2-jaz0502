An ATM asks for more trust in ten seconds than most machines this course
studies ask for all week: a card, a PIN, and a promise that the count in
your hand will match the number on the screen, with no receipt built to
survive an argument. That trust is exactly what a non-bank surcharge is
priced against.

## Outline

- the ATM as the point where a one-shot machine asks for the most trust:
  a card, a PIN, cash handed back without a receipt you can dispute
- withdrawal fees, network surcharges and cash-access deserts as decisions,
  not accidents of geography — a surcharge is a toll on trust, charged
  precisely where you have the least ability to shop around
- reading a fee schedule the way you'd read a contract, because it is one
