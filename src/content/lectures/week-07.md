A toll gate doesn't fail the way a jammed ticket machine fails — it
succeeds exactly as designed, and the failure is that you can't find out
the price until the bill arrives. That's not a UI bug. It's a concession
contract wearing a transponder.

## Outline

- e-tolling as the clearest case this semester of a business model, not a
  usability failure, showing up as a machine: the price genuinely can't be
  known until after you've already paid it
- reading a concession contract to find the incentive a toll gate's
  interface was built to protect — a minimum-revenue guarantee behaves very
  differently from a straight per-kilometre fee, and the gate can't tell you
  which one it's enforcing
- what "dynamic pricing" is doing when the driver has no way to see it
  coming
