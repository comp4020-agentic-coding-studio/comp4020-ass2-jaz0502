## Outline

- the captive user at their most vulnerable: unwell, in pain, or supporting
  someone who is, with no realistic option to walk away
- what a check-in terminal assumes about literacy, language and composure —
  and what it costs a person who doesn't match the assumption
- efficiency gains for the institution against the burden shifted onto the
  person least able to carry it
