## Outline

- a charging kiosk is captive twice over: to the location, and to whichever
  operator's app you happen to have installed
- roaming fees and account requirements as a standards war between
  operators, settled by charging the driver rather than resolving it
- why "just use the app" is doing a lot of quiet work in this sentence

## The argument

An EV charging kiosk makes you captive twice: first to whichever location
happens to have a free bay, and then to whichever operator built that
particular kiosk, whether or not you have their app, their account, or
their card. Two competing standards could have resolved this the way
payment networks eventually did, by agreeing to interoperate — but
resolving it wouldn't let either operator keep the customers roaming fees
currently lock in.

"Just use the app" sounds like a minor inconvenience, said once. Said at
every charger, by a different app each time, it's actually the cost of an
unresolved standards war being charged directly to the driver, one
download and one account at a time, instead of being absorbed by the
operators who left it unresolved.

**Question to carry:** if two operators can't agree on a standard, why is
the incompatibility always priced into the driver's bill rather than the
operators' margins?
