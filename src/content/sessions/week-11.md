## Before the tutorial

No fieldwork required this week — the case is confronting enough that it's
handled as a shared discussion rather than individual assignment.

## In the tutorial

We work through pay-toilet and turnstile kiosks as the starkest version of
this course's whole argument: no alternative, real time pressure, real
embarrassment, and — where findable — a franchise contract sitting behind
the machine. The discussion turns on whether "worse" and "more profitable"
are actually the same claim here.

## Afterwards

If this week's machine unsettled you, that's the point — bring the same
scrutiny to your own final project category, including the parts that
don't feel dramatic.
