## Before the tutorial

Nothing to prepare — this week's machine is provided in the room, or you use
your phone to pull up a photo of one you've used.

## In the tutorial

Groups teardown a vending-machine transaction into its individual decision
points: read the options, choose, pay, wait, retrieve, recover from a jam.
"Simple" machines turn out to hide as many silent decision points as
complicated ones — this week is about learning to see them.

## Afterwards

Keep your group's teardown format; you'll reuse the same decomposition
method on a machine of your own choosing for Assignment 1.
