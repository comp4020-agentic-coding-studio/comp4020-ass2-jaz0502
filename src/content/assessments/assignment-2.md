## The brief

> Pick one machine's worst habit, and show who is paid, protected, or made
> more efficient by it staying that way.

This is the semester's recurring question, made into a single piece of work:
not "is this bad," but "who benefits when it stays bad." A convincing incentive
map names a specific party — an operator, a franchise holder, a vendor's
contract terms — and backs the claim with something more than intuition:
a fee schedule, a maintenance pattern, a support policy, a contract clause if
you can find one.

## What you submit

A one-page incentive map (diagram or structured prose, your choice) plus a
written case (600–900 words) that states the claim, the evidence for it, and
the strongest argument against it that you could find.
